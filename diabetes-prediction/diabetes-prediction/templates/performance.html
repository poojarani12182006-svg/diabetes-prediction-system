Reading the metrics

Accuracy — overall share of correct predictions across both classes.

Precision — of everyone predicted diabetic, how many actually were.

Recall — of everyone who was actually diabetic, how many the model caught.

F1-score — the harmonic mean of precision and recall, used here to pick the deployed model.
